The studio

About us

NEW RM DESIGN SRL is an interior design company based in the historic centre of Naples.

Careful planning, clear communication

We work with private clients, small businesses and property owners who want a space that is comfortable, practical and well made. Every project starts with a conversation and a visit to the site.

Our studio sits in the Sanità district, close to the workshops, stone yards and furniture makers we like to work with. That proximity keeps decisions fast and materials honest.

We prepare complete documentation, so quotes are comparable and builders know what to deliver. We stay involved on site until the work is finished.

Services

Take one service on its own or the whole project from concept to completion.

01

Interior design

Concept, mood, materials and colour for the whole interior, drawn to scale.

  • Concept boards and 3D visuals
  • Material and finish selection
02

Space planning

Layouts that improve flow, daylight, storage and use of every square metre.

  • Measured survey and floor plans
  • Furniture layouts and options
03

Furniture & lighting

Sourcing and specification of furniture, lighting and textiles, including made-to-measure pieces from local workshops.

  • Supplier shortlists and quotes
  • Lighting plans
04

Renovation management

Schedules, trades and site visits handled by one point of contact.

  • Technical drawings and bills of quantities
  • Progress reports and snagging
05

Commercial fit-out

Shops, offices and hospitality spaces designed around staff routines and customer journeys.

  • Branded interiors
  • Phased openings
